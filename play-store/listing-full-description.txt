Autografr gets you an autograph signed by hand, on a photo, by the celebrity themselves.

Fans send a request with a photo and a short message. The celebrity draws their signature right on the photo. The signed photo goes into your collection.

FOR FANS
Browse featured celebrities and trending autographs.
Send an autograph request with your own photo, from the camera or your gallery.
Track your requests from sent to signed.
Keep every signed photo in your collection.
List a signed photo in the marketplace, or find one someone else listed.
Share signed photos to your social media.

FOR CELEBRITIES
Only verified celebrities can sign, so nobody can sign under someone else's name.
Work through requests in a queue.
Sign with 5 brushes (pen, marker, calligraphy, glow and eraser), with undo, colors and sizes.
Every signature is drawn by you, on that photo, for that fan.

LISTING HELP
When you list a signed photo, you can tap Suggest with Gemini to draft a title and description. It only runs when you tap it, and you can edit or discard the draft.

PRIVACY
No ads. No tracking. No analytics.
The app only sees the photos you pick, through Android's photo picker.
Photos are re-encoded on your phone before upload, which strips GPS location.
You can delete your account in the app any time (My Profile, menu, Delete account).
