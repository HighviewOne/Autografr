Autografr Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Autografr is an Android app where verified celebrities sign photos by hand and fans collect and trade the signed photos. The app is published by MirarVer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

Questions or requests: apps@mirarver.com.

Information we collect

WhatDetailsWhy
Account informationEmail address, password (handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see it), display name, and whether you are a fan or a celebrity.To create your account, sign you in, and show your name to other users.
ProfileOptional profile photo and bio. For celebrities: category, verification status, autograph price, and social links you add.To show your public profile in the app.
Photos and signaturesPhotos you take or choose, the signature you draw, and the finished signed photo with its thumbnail.This is the core of the app: requesting, signing, collecting and listing autographs.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or advertising ID. The app has no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking. When you upload a photo, the app re-encodes it on your device, which removes embedded metadata such as GPS location.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: only when you choose to take a photo in the app.
  • Photos: the app uses Android's photo picker, so it can only see the photos you choose to share with it, not your whole library.
  • Internet: to sync your account and photos.

AI writing suggestions

When creating a listing, you can tap Suggest with Gemini. Only then, a reduced copy of that one signed photo and the signer's name are sent to Google's Gemini model (through Firebase AI Logic) to draft a title and description. The suggestion is a draft you can edit or discard. Google processes this request as our service provider under the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service. Nothing is sent unless you tap the button.

Who can see your information

  • Other signed-in Autografr users can see your display name, profile photo and bio, signed photos you list or own, and listing details. Autograph requests are visible to the fan who sent them and the celebrity who received them.
  • Service providers: we use Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, App Check and AI Logic), operated by Google LLC, to store and process data on our behalf. See Firebase privacy and security.
  • Legal reasons: we may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, property or safety of our users or others.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest on Google's servers. Access is limited by security rules so that, for example, only you can change your profile and only the owner of a photo can list it.

How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, photos and requests within 30 days. Signed photos that another user has bought belong to their collection and may stay visible there with the signer's name. We may keep transaction records longer where the law requires it.

Your choices and deleting your account

  • You can edit your profile and remove your listings in the app at any time.
  • To delete your account in the app, open My Profile, tap the ⋮ menu, choose Delete account and enter your password. This deletes your profile and sign-in right away; your photos and requests are removed within 30 days.
  • If you can't use the app, email apps@mirarver.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my account". We will confirm by email once deletion is complete. Full steps: Delete your Autografr account.
  • You can also ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct it, at the same address.

If you live in California, the EU, the UK or another place with privacy laws, you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your information. Contact us and we will respond within the time the law requires.

Children

Autografr is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
