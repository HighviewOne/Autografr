Delete your Autografr account

Autografr, published by MirarVer

In the app

  1. Open Autografr and sign in.
  2. Tap your profile icon (top right) to open My Profile.
  3. Tap the ⋮ menu, then Delete account.
  4. Enter your password and tap Delete.

Your profile and sign-in are deleted immediately and you are signed out.

By email

If you can't use the app, email apps@mirarver.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete the account and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Right away: your profile (name, email, bio, social links), your list of blocked users, and your sign-in.
  • Within 30 days: your profile photo, your photos and signatures, unsold signed photos and listings, and your autograph requests and their messages.

What we keep

  • Signed photos that another user bought stay in the buyer's collection, with the signer's name.
  • Transaction records (what was bought, the amount and the date) may be kept longer where the law requires it.

More detail is in the privacy policy. Questions: apps@mirarver.com.
