How it works

Three taps between a fan and an autograph that can never be faked.

STEP 01

Open your pass

Every fan has a unique QR. Show it to a celebrity in person, or send it to one through the app for a daily drop.

STEP 02

The handshake

Their phone scans yours. That mutual scan is what makes the autograph real — proof of presence, not just a signature on a screen.

STEP 03

Yours, forever

The card is signed, sealed, and numbered. It lives in your collection — tradeable, frameable, permanently authenticated.

For collectors

A shelf you can actually trust.

Every card is a numbered edition with a permanent record of when, where, and who. Flip it over to see the certificate. Tilt it to see the foil. No more "is this real?"

  • Numbered edition · 1-of-1, rare, or open run
  • Tamper-proof handshake · both phones must scan
  • Resale on the marketplace · provenance follows the card
